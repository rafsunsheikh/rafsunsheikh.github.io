**Timeline:** July 2026

- **Project Description:** ClassCaption translates a lecture in real time, fully offline, over the classroom Wi-Fi. The lecturer speaks English, and students see live Chinese and Vietnamese captions, with speech, on their own phones. No internet connection or cloud service is needed. Built with Python, Flask and Whisper.

- **Project Github Link:** [ClassCaption](https://github.com/rafsunsheikh/ClassCaption)
