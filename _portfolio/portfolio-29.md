**Timeline:** July 2024 - August 2026

- **Project Description:** A hands-on record of learning blockchain development, from single-file Solidity contracts to Hardhat projects with tests, React front ends with ethers.js, and an ERC-721 real estate dApp with an escrow contract. It ends with a static security review of an ERC-20 stablecoin. A companion beginner tutorial shows how to build a first dApp in under 20 minutes: deploy a simple election contract to a local Ganache chain with Remix, then read and update it from a web page with web3.js.
