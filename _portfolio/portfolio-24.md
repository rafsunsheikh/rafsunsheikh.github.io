**Timeline:** May 2026 - June 2026

- **Project Description:** An open reference built around five questions every data project asks: what kind of data is this, where does it come from, how do industries shape it, how do I clean it, and how do I show it. It has more than 60 chapters with short summaries and decision tables, plus Jupyter notebooks that walk through cleaning and visualisation with pandas, matplotlib, seaborn and plotly.

- **Project Github Link:** [data-scientists-handbook](https://github.com/rafsunsheikh/data-scientists-handbook)
