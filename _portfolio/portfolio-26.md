**Timeline:** August 2025

- **Project Description:** Maps the main themes in 348 blockchain research papers using LDA topic modelling on their titles, abstracts and keywords. It compares models with different numbers of topics using c_v coherence, and shows the results with pyLDAvis and word clouds. Topics found include supply chains, healthcare data sharing, real estate and energy. Built with Python, NLTK, Gensim and pyLDAvis.
