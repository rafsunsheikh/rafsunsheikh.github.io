**Timeline:** June 2025 - August 2025

- **Project Description:** Interactive "what if" tools for university planning teams, covering the QS World, Employability and Sustainability rankings. You adjust the scores for each QS indicator, the app applies the QS weights to get an overall score, and a random forest model trained on published QS data estimates the resulting rank. Built with Python, Streamlit, pandas and scikit-learn.

- **Project Github Link:** [QS-Ranking-Apps](https://github.com/rafsunsheikh/QS-Ranking-Apps)
