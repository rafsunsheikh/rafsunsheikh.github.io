**Timeline:** June 2026

- **Project Description:** RegConflict is an expert-annotated benchmark for detecting conflicts between financial regulations from different jurisdictions. It pairs a four-class typology of conflict types with about 250 labelled regime pairs and a curated corpus of 247 regulatory documents. It also includes reproducible baselines, from random predictors and zero-shot prompted LLMs to a fine-tuned cross-encoder.

- **Project Github Link:** [RegConflict](https://github.com/rafsunsheikh/RegConflict)

- **Dataset:** [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rafsunsheikh/RegConflict) · [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21007776)
