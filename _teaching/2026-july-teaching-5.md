- Lecturing third-year Bachelor of Business Analytics students in Semester 2, 2026, at the Newcastle City Precinct (blended learning).
- Course topics:
    - Introduction to cybersecurity in business, information safety and contemporary cybersecurity principles
    - Data, network and application security, and the core technologies that provide cybersecurity for business
    - Designing cybersecurity solutions and the challenges involved
    - Human aspects of cybersecurity, including social engineering, and their managerial implications
    - AI and cybersecurity technologies, and contemporary cybersecurity applications
    - Cybersecurity project management in business, case studies of modern solutions, and future technologies
- Students learn to evaluate core cybersecurity principles in business operations, assess cyber risks, respond to incidents to keep a business running, and build cybersecurity strategies aligned with organisational goals, finishing with a strategy for a real-world business case.
- Total number of participants is 27 students.
